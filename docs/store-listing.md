## English

**Name:** One More Move (Bir Hamle Daha)
**Subtitle (30):** Every move changes everything
**Category:** Games › Puzzle · **Rating:** 4+ / Everyone

**Promotional text (170):** One move flips every gate and pushes your echo the other way. Think through all of its
consequences across 30 handcrafted puzzles.

**Description:**
One More Move is a calm, deep logic puzzle on a small board.

• Every successful move opens or closes every gate. Be there at the right moment, or wait a turn.
• Your echo moves exactly opposite to you and stays put when blocked; steer it against the walls.
• 30 levels, from first steps to mastery levels with several plausible routes.
• Every level's shortest solution is proven by a solver. Find the fewest moves for three stars.
• Unlimited undo, step-by-step hints, no timers.
• Keyboard, gamepad and touch; screen reader support, large text and reduced motion.
• No ads, no tracking.

**Keywords (100):** puzzle,logic,strategy,gates,echo,moves,brain,calm,minimal,turn-based
